Your privacy is important to Home Revivo. When you visit our website or request a quote, you share personal details with us. Here is a simple breakdown of what we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse the Site, create an account, or submit a project enquiry, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and property street address or ZIP/postal code.
  • Project Information: Home improvement category (e.g., Roofing, HVAC, Solar, Kitchen/Bath Remodeling), project timeline, budget preferences, and property details.
  • Account Credentials: Username, encrypted password, and enquiry history if you register via our Sign-Up page.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, referring URLs, and pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Match your home improvement project with qualified local Service Providers;
  • Communicate with you regarding your quote requests, account updates, or customer support enquiries;
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our calculators, quote forms, and website performance; and
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent enquiries, spam, or unauthorized access.

3. Sharing Information With Service Providers

Core Platform Disclosure: When you submit a quote request or project enquiry on Home Revivo, you explicitly instruct and authorize us to share your contact information and project specifications with matched third-party Service Providers so they can contact you with estimates.

Once your information is shared with a Service Provider, their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies. We may also share data with trusted infrastructure vendors (such as hosting, analytics, and email/SMS delivery providers) who assist us in operating the Site under strict confidentiality obligations.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Home Revivo uses cookies and similar local storage technologies to keep you signed in, remember your form progress, and analyze site traffic. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though certain features of the quote-matching form may not function properly.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards (including HTTPS encryption) to protect your personal data against unauthorized access or disclosure. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill your project enquiries, maintain your account, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete project or contact details;
  • Request deletion of your account and personal records; or
  • Opt out of promotional emails or SMS messages at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions or replying "STOP".

7. Contact Us

To exercise your privacy rights or ask questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

  • Website: https://home-improvement-nu.vercel.app/
  • Email: privacy@homerevivo.com
